About Scalpx

Root-cause
hair science.

We started Scalpx because great hair care ignores the one thing that matters most: the skin it grows from.

Our story

Hair is the result. The scalp is the cause.

Product-first routines often treat symptoms — dryness, shedding, flakes — while the scalp environment stays unbalanced. Scalpx flips the order: analyse first, then treat what the scalp actually needs.

Our protocols are designed with textured and afro hair in mind, where tension, dryness and protective styling make scalp care essential.
